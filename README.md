# Trades Office Starter

Free starter for residential trades companies: HVAC, plumbing, electrical,
remodeling.

Two things it does from day one:

**Sort your missed calls.** Paste your voicemails and get a callback order
with the dangerous stuff on top. It knows a gas smell outranks a flooded
laundry room, and both outrank a mini-split quote.

**Your Friday numbers.** Connect QuickBooks and Google once (guided, one
afternoon) and get cash, overdue invoices, and next week's schedule in a
three-minute plain-English report every Friday.

Rules it never breaks: nothing sends without your OK, it never invents a
price, and life-safety calls get flagged for a human callback immediately.
